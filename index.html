About Us

Tradition & craft from
León, Guanajuato

At Casa Maximiliano we believe a fine leather shoe must carry a story of excellence. We personally source every hide in León, Guanajuato — the leather capital of Mexico and home to its finest tanneries and cobblers.

Each pair is made using traditional goodyear-welt methods, where master hands cut, last and hand-finish every detail. We do not make products in series — we craft heirloom footwear built to last generations.
